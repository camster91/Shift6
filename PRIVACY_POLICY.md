# Privacy Policy — Shift6

**Effective Date:** March 17, 2026  
**App:** Shift6 - Bodyweight Fitness  
**Developer:** Ashbi Design (cameron@ashbi.ca)

---

## Summary

Shift6 is a local fitness app. **We collect no personal data. Everything stays on your device.**

---

## Data Collection

Shift6 does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal information:

- ❌ No account required
- ❌ No analytics or tracking
- ❌ No advertising
- ❌ No data sent to external servers

---

## Data Storage

All workout data, progress, and settings are stored **locally on your device only**.

- Your data never leaves your device
- Uninstalling the app removes all data
- We have no access to your fitness data

---

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Notifications | Workout reminders (optional) |
| Vibration/Haptics | Workout feedback |

---

## Children's Privacy

Shift6 does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts or data collection of any kind.
