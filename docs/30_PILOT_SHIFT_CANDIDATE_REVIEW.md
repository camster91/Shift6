## Proposed comparison boundaries to settle in review

- Push-up: a single attempt's valid rep count, with the same reviewed floor variant, assistance and setup at baseline and follow-up. Different variants remain separate observation series. A genuine measured zero requires a qualifying attempt and produces absolute change without a percentage; “not yet able,” skipped and deferred are distinct states.
- Plank: duration in seconds under the same reviewed start/end technique and surface/setup. A timer running alone does not establish technique or an assessment. The plan's timed sets do not silently become performance observations.
- Bench: an actual qualifying completed five-rep load, stored with entered unit and normalized for compatible comparison. Reviewer-approved form, completion and source criteria must determine whether a routine workout set qualifies; a logged set alone does not. Changing the rep count, implement, assistance or setup changes comparability; an estimated one-rep maximum cannot become a measured five-rep result. No maximal test or fixed loading target is prescribed by this packet.
- All three: target choice is an individual goal, never a six-week guarantee. Sessions completed, adherence, calendar week, latest qualifying measurement, best and estimates remain separate. Early attainment does not increase a target automatically. A postponed or declined final test still permits an honest Week 6 review and next choice.

## How the human review happens

For each proposed Shift, the product owner prepares the exact immutable program version, every referenced exercise record, baseline/final-test instructions, progression and interruption rules, intended user and equipment, and all user-facing copy. A qualified fitness-content reviewer examines the complete six-week plan rather than only its catalogue card. The reviewer walks through assessment, a normal session, a missed-week return, substitutions, a stop/discomfort case and each end-of-block outcome. They compare each instruction and measurement against what the app actually records and displays, then document findings and required revisions. Engineering checks structure and references; representative native builds are checked separately for usable instructions and logging. After revisions, the reviewer signs off on the **resulting exact versions**, or records why approval is withheld.

The durable record needs reviewer identity and relevant qualification, date, source program/template/protocol and exercise version IDs, review reference, checklist findings, resolved changes and final disposition. A later material change to movement, targets, audience, safety, assessment or progression needs renewed review. Generated exercise media, if added, has its own human technique and provenance review. We cannot self-certify biomechanical correctness through tests or mark draft content approved without this evidence.

## Review decisions and evidence required

1. Intended audience, prerequisites, exclusions and realistic equipment/setup, with a plan/add-on decision. The main Shift has one primary outcome; other movements support it.
2. An exact six-week executable version: session sequence and frequency, training targets/rest, progression and difficulty response, Week 6 review, and reasonable repeat/maintenance path. A source version may need a new immutable goal-specific version. Do not edit completed snapshots in place.
3. Written, versioned baseline/final assessment instructions: technique and stop conditions, non-maximal or manual entry option where appropriate, “not yet able” and deferred routes, comparable variant/setup rules, and which ordinary workout entries may qualify. Do not infer a result from workout completion.
4. Equipment substitution matrix stating when a substitution trains the goal but **does not** yield a comparable measurement. For bench, verify rack/spotting and alternate assessment route; do not infer safety clearance from catalogue metadata.
5. Missed-day, pause, resume, repeat-week, reduced session and return-to-training choices with reviewed load/volume boundaries. Any shorter session needs essential preparation, omitted work, progression eligibility and an honest distinction from an unplanned partial session; otherwise offer reschedule/pause. Calendar position follows real dates; progression follows actual performance. Stop/discomfort remains separate from ordinary difficulty and never triggers catch-up.
6. User-facing goal and outcome copy for target reached early, improved but unmet, unchanged, lower, non-comparable, postponed and no final test; maintenance and a planned break are valid next choices. Coach may explain, but no paid provider call is required.
7. Exact `programId`, immutable `programVersionId`, all referenced exercise IDs and variant/protocol IDs, reviewer identity/qualification, review date/reference and any resolved feedback. A material content change needs renewed version-bound approval. Review exercise technique, safety and media provenance under [exercise review](18_EXERCISE_CONTENT_REVIEW.md) and the whole plan under [program review](19_PROGRAM_CONTENT_REVIEW.md).
