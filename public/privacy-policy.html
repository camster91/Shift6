Privacy Policy for Armor

Last Updated: June 16, 2026

Summary: Armor is local-first by default. All your workout data lives on your device, and the app works fully offline. Optional cloud sync (workouts, 1RMs, preferences) is opt-in — it activates only when you create an account. We do not collect, sell, or share data with third parties.

Introduction

Armor (formerly "Shift6") is a fitness application developed by Cameron Ashley and published under the brand name Armor. This Privacy Policy describes how the Armor mobile application (iOS App Store and Google Play) and the web app at getshift6.com handle information. We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our app.

By installing or using Armor, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device (always)

Armor stores the following data in your device's local storage (localStorage on the web, device storage on iOS and Android) to provide its functionality:

  • Workout History: Records of completed exercises, dates, sets, reps, and performance metrics.
  • Progress Data: Your advancement through the 6-week program, current cycle / week / day, and cycle completion count.
  • Estimated 1RMs: Your one-rep-max estimates for each tracked exercise.
  • Personal Records: Your best performances for each exercise.
  • Daily Habits: Markers for the four daily longevity habits (balance drill, lunch walk, dinner walk, evening floor work).
  • Streak Data: Workout streak counts, minimum-viable-day (MVD) days, and freeze allowance.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as theme (dark/light), unit (lbs/kg), equipment track (full gym / home gym), and reminder schedule.

This local data:

  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is never uploaded to any server unless you opt in to cloud sync (see below)
  • Is never shared with any third party
  • Is never used for advertising
  • Is fully under your control at all times

Optional Cloud Sync (opt-in account feature)

Armor offers an optional cloud sync feature that lets you back up your data and access it from multiple devices. This feature is off by default and requires you to explicitly create an account.

If you create an Armor account and choose to sync, we store:

  • Your email address (for login only — never used for marketing or shared)
  • A bcrypt hash of your password (the plain-text password is never stored)
  • Your workout history, 1RMs, preferences, streak data, and habits (the same data stored locally)
  • A monotonically increasing revision number used for conflict resolution
  • The IP address of login events, retained for 30 days for security (brute-force protection)

We do not store your real name, physical address, phone number, date of birth, payment information (there is no paid tier), device identifiers, advertising IDs, location data, contacts, photos, microphone input, or any third-party tracking data.

All cloud-sync traffic is over HTTPS. The sync endpoint is sync.getshift6.com. Authentication is via a JWT issued at login and stored in your device's localStorage (not a cookie).

You can delete your account and all cloud-stored data at any time from the Account tab. Deletion is permanent; we do not retain backups of deleted accounts. Your local data on the device remains intact.

Migration From Shift6

Armor can read the local-storage keys left by the previous Shift6 app on first launch. Migration happens entirely on-device: the old shift6_settings and shift6_onboarding_done keys are read, the values are translated to the new armor_* keys, and the old keys are left in place (you can clear them via your browser's site data tools). No data is sent off-device during migration.

No Internet Required for Core Features

The app's core features (onboarding, workout logging, progress charts, periodization, plate math, rest timer, daily habits) all work fully offline. Network requests occur only in the following cases:

  • You create or sign in to a cloud-sync account (HTTPS to sync.getshift6.com)
  • The service worker checks for a new app version (cache-first PWA update)
  • You build the app with analytics enabled (see below)

Analytics

The app ships with a stub Google Analytics 4 integration. Analytics are off by default. The SDK is included in the bundle but is never initialized unless the app is built with a VITE_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID environment variable set. Without that variable, no analytics events are sent, no cookies are set, and the SDK adds nothing to the runtime.

Third-Party Services

The app bundles the following libraries (no runtime fetches):

  • React, React DOM (UI framework)
  • lucide-react (icon set)
  • vite-plugin-pwa (service worker for offline caching)
  • react-ga4 (analytics SDK; only initialized if you set the env var above)

The marketing landing page at getshift6.com uses Google Fonts. The PWA itself does not.

No Account Required

Armor does not require you to create an account to use the app. The "Account" tab in the bottom navigation only appears after you have created an account. The default local-only experience is fully functional.

Data Deletion

You have complete and immediate control over your data:

  • Local Data: Open Settings → Factory Reset to clear all armor_* keys from local storage on the current device.
  • Cloud Data: Sign in, then go to Account → Delete Account to permanently remove all data we hold for your account, including the account record itself.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all associated data from your device. Cloud-stored data, if any, is retained until you delete your account.

Children's Privacy

Armor does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app does not require any personal information to function. The app is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed at children.

Permissions

Armor may request the following device permissions:

  • Vibration / Haptics: To provide haptic feedback during workouts (optional).
  • Notifications: To send locally scheduled workout and habit reminders (optional, user-initiated).
  • Status Bar / Splash Screen: Cosmetic configuration to match the app's dark theme.

No permission is used to collect, transmit, or share data.

International Users

Armor is designed to comply with international data protection regulations, including:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation — European Union)
  • CCPA / CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act — United States)
