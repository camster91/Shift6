### Privacy Policy for Shift6

**Last Updated: February 26, 2026**

#### Introduction

Shift6 ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") is a fitness application developed and published by Ashbi. This Privacy Policy describes how the Shift6 mobile application (available on Google Play as com.shift6.app) handles information. We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our App.

By installing or using Shift6, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

#### Summary

**Shift6 does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** All information generated by the App is stored exclusively on your device and is never sent to any external server.

#### Information We Do NOT Collect

Shift6 does **not** collect, access, or process any of the following:

- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Accounts, usernames, or passwords
- Location or GPS data
- Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
- IP addresses or network information
- Photos, camera, or microphone data
- Contacts, call logs, or SMS
- Financial or payment information
- Health data from device sensors
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Crash reports transmitted to external services
- Any data sent over the internet

#### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Shift6 stores the following data in your device's local storage (localStorage) to provide its functionality:

- **Workout History:** Records of completed exercises, dates, sets, reps, and performance metrics.
- **Progress Data:** Your advancement through the 6-week program, sprint records, and exercise levels.
- **Personal Records:** Your best performances for each exercise.
- **Goals:** Your 6-week goal targets and weekly progress toward them.
- **App Preferences:** Settings such as theme (dark/light), audio preferences, training schedule, rest timer configuration, and accessibility options.
- **Custom Programs:** Any workout programs you have created or modified.
- **Body Metrics (optional):** Weight and body measurements, only if you choose to enter them.

This data:
- Is stored **only** on your device
- Is **never** uploaded to any server
- Is **never** shared with any third party
- Is **never** used for advertising, analytics, or profiling
- Is **fully under your control** at all times

#### No Internet Connection Required

Shift6 functions entirely offline. The App does not make network requests, connect to APIs, or communicate with any external service during normal use. The only network activity occurs if your device's app store checks for application updates, which is handled by Google Play and not by the App itself.

#### No Third-Party Services

Shift6 does not integrate any third-party SDKs, libraries, or services that collect user data. There are no:

- Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
- Advertising networks or ad SDKs
- Crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
- Social media integrations
- Attribution or tracking tools
- Cloud storage or synchronization services

#### No Account Required

Shift6 does not require you to create an account, register, log in, or provide any personal information to use the App. All features are available immediately upon installation.

#### Data Export and Backup

Shift6 provides a built-in data export feature that allows you to save your workout data as a file to your device. This export is performed locally and does not involve any server communication. You may also import previously exported data to restore your progress. You are solely responsible for any exported files.

#### Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and immediate control over it:

- **In-App Reset:** Use the "Factory Reset" option within the App's settings to erase all workout data and restore default settings.
- **Uninstall:** Removing the App from your device will permanently delete all data associated with it.
- **Clear App Data:** You may clear the App's data through your device's system settings at any time.

No data persists on any external server after any of these actions, because no data was ever sent to an external server in the first place.

#### Children's Privacy

Shift6 does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. However, the App is intended for general audiences interested in fitness and is not specifically directed at children.

#### Permissions

Shift6 may request the following device permissions:

- **Vibration:** To provide haptic feedback during workouts (optional).
- **Wake Lock:** To keep the screen on during active workout sessions.
- **Notifications:** To send locally scheduled workout reminders (optional, user-initiated).

No permission is used to collect, transmit, or share data.

#### Google Play Data Safety

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety section requirements:

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | No |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | Not applicable -- no data is transmitted |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | Yes -- Factory Reset feature and standard uninstall |
| Does your app share user data with third parties? | No |

**Data Safety Declaration:** "No data collected. No data shared."

#### International Users

Because Shift6 does not collect or transmit any personal data, it inherently complies with international data protection regulations including:

- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation -- European Union)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act -- United States)
- **PIPEDA** (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act -- Canada)
- **LGPD** (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados -- Brazil)

Since no personal data is collected, traditional data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction of processing) are inherently satisfied. You already possess and control all of your data on your device.

#### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the App after any modifications constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

If we ever make a material change to our data practices (such as introducing data collection), we will notify users through an in-app notice before the change takes effect.

#### Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices, please contact us:

- **Email:** support@ashbi.ca
- **Website:** https://getshift6.com
